How it works

A guided onboarding journey

You will see what is needed at every step before you share sensitive information.

01

Verify your identity

Enter your identification and confirm access to the registered contact.

02

Find eligible facilities

View facilities associated with your verified identity and registry record.

03

Choose facilities

Select the facility or facilities you want to onboard.

04

Choose a token package

Review the available package, token quantity, price and applicable terms.

05

Review and submit

Check your choices, accept the terms and send your request. No signing step is needed.

06

Follow your next steps

Sign in to Customer Experience to review your quote and any order, invoice or activation steps.
