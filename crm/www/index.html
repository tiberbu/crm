All token rules (12)
  1. Prepaid. Tokens are credited to the customer account once payment has been confirmed.
  2. Monthly system access. A fixed {{ amount(offer.access_tokens) }}-token system access charge is deducted at the beginning of each funded month.
  3. No expiry. Purchased tokens do not expire while the customer account remains active.
  4. Transaction usage. Service-point tokens are deducted for each applicable completed transaction according to the prevailing token schedule.
  5. Patient billing. An additional 1 token per {{ cur }} {{ amount(offer.bill_value_per_token) }} of the total patient bill, rounded up, applies.
  6. Cumulative usage. Where an encounter attracts both a service-point charge and a patient-bill charge, both deductions apply.
  7. Insufficient balance. When the token balance reaches zero or becomes negative, system access is limited until more tokens are credited.
  8. Easy reactivation. Full system access resumes once enough tokens have been credited.
  9. Top-ups. Top up at any time at the prevailing package or agreed commercial rate, with a minimum top-up of {{ amount(offer.min_tokens or 1000) }} tokens.
  10. OPD protection. OPD-only facilities are not charged tokens for inpatient admissions or medical/surgical procedures.
  11. Pricing. All prices are in Kenya Shillings (KES). Statutory taxes are added where required.
  12. Commercial protection. Token allocations and service applicability may only be varied through a written commercial agreement.
