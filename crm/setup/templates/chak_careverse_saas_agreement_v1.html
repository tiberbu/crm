1. Contract information

1.1 Parties

This Software-as-a-Service Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into by and between:

TIBERBU HEALTHNET LIMITED, a company incorporated in the Republic of Kenya, with its registered office at 4th Avenue Towers, 18th Floor, 4th Ngong Avenue off Ngong Road, Nairobi (the “Provider” or “Tiberbu”);

{{ customer.name }}, being the healthcare facility or legal entity identified in this Agreement (the “Customer”), with the facility profile shown below; and

{{ network.legal_name }} (the “Network Partner”), being the network or coordinating organisation through which the Customer is participating in this CareverseHIMS programme. The Network Partner’s role is limited to the coordination responsibilities expressly described in this Agreement and does not make it the owner or licensor of CareverseHIMS.

Tiberbu, the Customer and the Network Partner are each a “Party” and together the “Parties”.

1.2 Recitals

  • Tiberbu owns, operates and licenses the proprietary cloud-based CareverseHIMS hospital management information system.
  • The Customer operates the facility identified in this Agreement and wishes to subscribe to CareverseHIMS with implementation, training and support.
  • The Network Partner supports the Customer’s onboarding and coordination under its applicable network framework.
  • The Parties intend this Agreement and its schedules to be the complete agreement for the Customer’s facility subscription.

1.3 Definitions and interpretation

“CareverseHIMS” means Tiberbu’s hosted hospital management information system, including its software, updates, documentation and related services. “Customer Data” means data entered into, generated through, stored in or processed by CareverseHIMS for the Customer. “Services” means the subscription, hosting, support, implementation, training, maintenance and related services described in this Agreement. “Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Kenya.

References to laws include amendments and subordinate legislation. The headings are for convenience only. The schedules form part of this Agreement.

1.4 Order of precedence

If documents conflict, the main body of this Agreement prevails, followed by the schedules, then any signed quotation, order form or statement of work incorporated by reference. The facility-specific pricing shown in Schedule B above prevails on the commercial amounts for this facility.

2. Commercial terms

2.1 Scope of Services

Tiberbu shall provide the CareverseHIMS subscription, hosting, embedded support, implementation, configuration and role-based training described in this Agreement. The Network Partner may assist with onboarding, facility engagement, training logistics and escalation coordination.

2.2 Subscription grant and permitted use

Subject to payment of the applicable Fees and compliance with this Agreement, Tiberbu grants the Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable and revocable right during the Subscription Term to access and use CareverseHIMS solely for the Customer’s internal healthcare operations at the facility identified in Schedule A. No ownership of the software is transferred.

2.3 Authorized users

The Customer shall ensure that only authorised users access CareverseHIMS, credentials are not shared, and access is removed promptly when a user leaves or no longer needs access.

2.4 Subscription term and renewal

The initial Subscription Term is sixty (60) months from the Effective Date unless terminated earlier under Clause 15. It renews for successive sixty (60) month periods unless either Party gives at least sixty (60) days’ written notice of non-renewal.

2.5 Facility-specific Fees and pricing

Schedule B above is the complete pricing schedule for this Agreement. It contains only the facility or facilities covered by this individual contract, their KEPH levels and the negotiated rates drawn from the applicable quotation. No prices for unrelated facility levels or other customers form part of this Agreement.

2.6 Taxes

All Fees are exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT), withholding tax and other applicable taxes, levies or duties imposed under Kenyan law. VAT is calculated from the configured Taxes and Charges template and added to the invoice at the applicable statutory rate. Where withholding tax is required, the Customer shall provide Tiberbu with the official withholding tax certificate.

2.7 Payment terms

Unless the quotation states otherwise, annual subscription Fees are payable in advance. Quarterly payment, where offered, is an instalment arrangement and does not reduce the annual commitment. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days. All payments shall be made in Kenya Shillings (KES), without deduction or set-off except as required by law.

2.8 Payment account and late payment

Payments must be made to the bank account designated by Tiberbu in writing. If an undisputed amount remains unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after its due date, Tiberbu may charge a late payment fee of five percent (5%) and, after written notice, suspend access until payment is received, while using reasonable efforts to minimise disruption to emergency patient care.

2.9 Network Partner role and authority

{{ network.legal_name }} may coordinate onboarding, facility engagement, training logistics, implementation tracking and escalation. It may not vary prices or payment terms, bind Tiberbu or the Customer, access Customer Data except as expressly authorised, or represent itself as the owner or licensor of CareverseHIMS.

3. Services description

3.1 CareverseHIMS

CareverseHIMS is a cloud-based hospital management information system integrating patient registration, clinical documentation, billing and financial management, pharmacy and inventory, reporting and analytics, and compliance administration.

3.2 Included functionality

Depending on the subscribed service scope, the Services may include patient registration and appointments, triage and nursing documentation, electronic medical records, diagnostics and procedures, billing, pharmacy and inventory, reporting, dashboards, audit support and payer workflows.

3.3 Third-party services

CareverseHIMS may integrate with payer, regulatory and diagnostic platforms where technically feasible. Availability, performance, acceptance and reimbursement outcomes of third-party platforms remain outside Tiberbu’s control.

3.4 Implementation, training and changes

Tiberbu shall provide or oversee implementation, configuration and role-based training in accordance with the agreed plan. Data migration and any work outside the agreed baseline scope are subject to a written change request and, where applicable, additional Fees.

4. Service levels and support

Tiberbu shall provide support in accordance with this Clause 4 and Schedule C. Embedded support is included in the subscription unless dedicated on-site support is agreed separately.

4.1 Availability

Tiberbu shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain the hosted production environment. The target availability is 99.5% monthly, excluding scheduled or emergency maintenance, customer-caused outages, connectivity or power failures outside the managed environment, third-party systems and force majeure.

4.2 Support channels

Support is available through the support portal at https://www.tiberbu.com/support, email at support@tiberbu.com and telephone at +254111043566 or +254111043555. Support matters should include the facility name, severity, business impact and actions already taken.

4.3 Severity and response targets

  • Critical: production unavailable or material business stoppage — initial response within one hour during covered support hours.
  • High: major functionality unavailable without a reasonable workaround — initial response within four business hours.
  • Medium: moderate impact with a workaround — initial response within one business day.
  • Low: minor issue, cosmetic concern or general guidance — initial response within two business days.

Scheduled maintenance will, where reasonably practicable, be notified in advance and performed outside core operating hours. Emergency maintenance may occur without prior notice to address a security, availability or regulatory risk.

5. Customer obligations

The Customer shall provide accurate implementation information, nominate operational, clinical, finance and technical contacts, cooperate with onboarding and training, maintain suitable devices and connectivity, manage users and access, and comply with applicable laws.

The Customer shall not reverse engineer CareverseHIMS, introduce malicious code, infringe third-party rights, submit fraudulent claims or resell access without Tiberbu’s written consent.

6. Data protection and privacy

Each Party shall comply with the Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable ODPC guidance. The Customer is the data controller for patient, staff and facility data. Tiberbu is the data processor and shall process Customer Data only on documented instructions, except where required by law. The Network Partner is not a controller or processor unless expressly authorised in writing and shall have only the minimum access needed for its coordination role.

Customer Data may include demographic, clinical, billing, claims, staff, access and audit information. Tiberbu shall apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, support data-subject requests where reasonably required, and notify the Customer without undue delay and within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of a confirmed personal data breach affecting Customer Data.

Tiberbu may use sub-processors to provide the Services, provided that equivalent data protection obligations apply and Tiberbu remains responsible for their performance. Cross-border transfers shall comply with Kenyan law.

7. Information security

Security is a shared responsibility. Tiberbu provides role-based access controls, encryption in transit and at rest within the managed environment, monitoring, backup, disaster recovery and security incident processes. The Customer is responsible for local devices, connectivity, physical security, passwords, user behaviour and timely de-provisioning.

8. Data ownership and records

The Customer owns Customer Data. Tiberbu owns CareverseHIMS, its software, architecture, documentation and de-identified aggregate operational metadata that does not identify the Customer or a data subject.

The Customer may export Customer Data in a commonly used structured format during the Subscription Term and for the applicable retention period. Following termination, Customer Data will be available on a read-only basis for seven (7) years unless law or a written agreement requires longer. Data archive during legally required retention is available without an additional fee; bespoke migration assistance may be separately priced.

9. Intellectual property

All intellectual property in CareverseHIMS remains with Tiberbu or its licensors. Customer-specific configuration and enhancements form part of the platform unless otherwise agreed in writing. The Customer receives only the limited use rights granted by this Agreement. Open-source components remain subject to their respective licences.

10. Confidentiality

Each Party shall protect the other Party’s non-public commercial, technical, financial, operational, security and patient-related information and use it only for the permitted purpose. Disclosure is permitted to personnel, advisers, authorised representatives or regulators who need to know and are bound by equivalent obligations. Confidentiality survives for five (5) years after termination; obligations for Health Data and trade secrets survive while the information remains confidential.

11. Warranties

Tiberbu warrants that, under normal authorised use, CareverseHIMS will materially perform in accordance with its Documentation and that professional services will be delivered with reasonable skill and care. Regulatory readiness features do not guarantee certification, approval or acceptance by SHA, DHA, HIE or another third party. Except as expressly stated, no implied warranty is given to the maximum extent permitted by Kenyan law.

12. Risk allocation

Each Party shall indemnify the other for direct losses arising from its breach of data protection or confidentiality obligations, gross negligence, wilful misconduct or fraud. Tiberbu shall defend qualifying intellectual-property claims arising from authorised use of CareverseHIMS. Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential loss. Subject to the exceptions below, aggregate liability is capped at the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event. Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or wilful misconduct.

13. Regulatory and sector compliance

The Customer remains responsible for facility licensing, clinical governance, lawful data collection, records management and compliance with Kenyan healthcare requirements. CareverseHIMS may support SHA, DHA, HIE and payer workflows, but external processing, approvals and system availability remain subject to the applicable regulator or payer.

14. Change management

Changes to scope, workflows, integrations, reports, training, deployment, timelines, pricing, payment structure, support entitlement or facility onboarding assumptions require a written change request. No variation is binding until confirmed in writing by Tiberbu and the Customer; the Network Partner may coordinate the request.

15. Termination

Either Party may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days’ written notice or for an uncured material breach after sixty (60) days’ notice. Tiberbu may terminate for undisputed non-payment after the applicable suspension and notice periods. Either Party may terminate immediately for insolvency. On termination, access ceases except for read-only data retrieval rights, outstanding amounts become payable, and each Party returns or destroys the other’s Confidential Information subject to legal retention obligations.

16. Legal and governance provisions

Notices shall be delivered by hand, registered post or email with confirmation of receipt to the addresses identified by the Parties. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without consent, except to an Affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets. Tiberbu may subcontract while remaining responsible for subcontractors. The Parties are independent contractors. This Agreement is the entire agreement, may be amended only in writing, is severable, may be executed in counterparts and may be signed electronically where permitted by law.

17. Dispute resolution and governing law

The Parties shall first attempt good-faith negotiation within fourteen (14) days of written notice of a dispute, then escalate to senior management and attempt mediation. An unresolved dispute shall be finally resolved by a sole arbitrator seated in Nairobi under the Arbitration Act, 1995 and the rules of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch). Kenyan courts have jurisdiction for interim relief and matters not subject to arbitration. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya.
